I study finite automata through co-lexicographic orders on their states,
and the co-lex width that such orders assign to automata and regular
languages. My [bachelor thesis](/papers/minisini-bsc-thesis.pdf) describes the Hasse
automaton, a canonical automaton that achieves the optimal width for a
given language, and characterizes the equivalences inducing minimum-state
deterministic automata of fixed width. In [@conf/ictcs/DAgostinoMP26] we
consider the non-deterministic width of regular languages in the unary
case, that is, for languages $L \subseteq a^*$ over a one-letter alphabet.
